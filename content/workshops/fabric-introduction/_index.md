A hands-on, end-to-end introduction to Microsoft Fabric, taking learners from raw data ingestion and storage through to modelling, reporting and AI-powered exploration. Prior analytics knowledge is helpful but not required. 

The six-hour course can be delivered in person over one day or virtually across three to four sessions. Client data can be incorporated, subject to additional chargeable preparation time.
